AI governance & autonomous-agent oversight

Two levels of governed AI oversight.

TENS is designed to watch autonomous work without silently taking over authority. The two packages below use the same evidence-first governance principles, but they differ in scope, integration depth, and the amount of external AI infrastructure TENS is responsible for monitoring.

How advanced activation works

Production governance is earned per integration.

1 · MapDocument the customer’s agents, systems, permissions and escalation rules.
2 · Connect read-only firstAdmit only the telemetry and records TENS needs to govern the environment.
3 · Adversarial acceptanceTest permission drift, loops, stale workers, retry overflow, pressure and recovery.
4 · ActivateEnable recurring governance only after the exact customer integration passes.

The Level-1 external-company canary passed the current governance control set. That does not automatically authorize TENS to control a customer’s systems; each connection receives its own authority boundary and acceptance test.
