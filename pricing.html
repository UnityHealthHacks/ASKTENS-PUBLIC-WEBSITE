How the pricing boundary works

Included usage is a hard boundary, not an invitation to an unexpected bill. TENS records jobs, documents, input volume and work units. When a plan reaches its included capacity, new costly work is held for a plan/capacity decision. Usage events remain auditable and do not create automatic overage charges.

Prices shown in USD per organization per month. Organization-specific integrations, regulated workflows, or systems requiring custom connectors remain subject to scoped onboarding and acceptance testing before production use.
