Evidence standards

What TENS may say

State the documented permit outcome, plan dimensions, conditions, and source pages. Distinguish authorizations from actual construction or compliance.

What TENS must not say

Do not claim all permits are final, that County approval is established, that seagrass was unharmed, or that a federal extension occurred without new evidence.

Unknowns awaiting separate records
  • Final Sarasota County special-exception/other local authorization status.
  • Whether the SPGP VI conditional federal continuation requirements were satisfied.
  • Actual constructed conditions, seagrass impacts, vessel clearance, and compliance with all conditions.
  • Full contents of the October 9, 2026 Sarasota County partial public-records release.
  • The conflicting Accela identifiers shown in County notices and its active petition listing.
