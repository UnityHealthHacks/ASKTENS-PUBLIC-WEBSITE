Public-record follow-up

When TENS cannot support an answer, the intended future workflow is to identify the evidence gap, obtain the relevant public records through the authorized chain, and notify the requester when verified material arrives. That autonomous public-request feature is not active in this public beta yet.

Facts first. Transparency always. Assumptions never. A missing public result is not proof that no responsive record exists.
