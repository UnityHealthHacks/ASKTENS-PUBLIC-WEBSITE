TENS for Business · Coming Soon

Put evidence-driven autonomy to work.

TENS is being developed as more than a public-records research system. Coming soon, business implementations can be designed around each organization’s specific needs, priorities, operating boundaries, and measurable goals for success. The architecture is designed for bounded autonomous operations: collecting information, organizing it, reconciling records, identifying gaps and contradictions, taking authorized actions, and preserving an auditable trail of what happened.

Coming Soon · AI Governance

Govern autonomous agents with evidence, boundaries and verification.

TENS is being developed to help organizations define what autonomous agents are allowed to do, monitor whether they stay inside those boundaries, preserve source and action provenance, verify important outputs through watchdogs and readback, surface conflicts or failures, and maintain durable audit receipts. Governance parameters can be designed around each organization’s systems, risks, policies and measurable goals.

Financial & audit operations

Reconcile invoices, purchase orders, contracts, payments and reimbursements; surface documented discrepancies for human review.

Construction & contracting

Track contracts, change orders, permits, inspections, schedules, deliverables, vendors and project records.

Property operations

Organize work orders, vendor records, maintenance, insurance documentation, budgets and administrative deadlines.

Document-intensive work

Build traceable chronologies, classify large record sets, locate missing material and preserve source provenance.

Logistics, fleet & marine

Coordinate maintenance records, inspections, licensing, parts, vendor invoices and recurring compliance documentation.

Business back office

Support bounded email, document, reporting, research, reconciliation and customer workflows with verification controls.

What makes TENS different

Answers are only part of the job.

The goal is a system that can show what it found, where the underlying evidence came from, what conflicts, what remains unknown, what authorized action it took, and the durable receipt supporting that work.
